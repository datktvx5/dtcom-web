DTCOM - KICH HOAT THAO LUAN DICH VU

1) Website da chen Utterances vao 4 trang dich vu.
2) De phan thao luan hoat dong:
   - Trong GitHub repo datktvx5/dtcom-web, vao Settings > General > Features va bat Issues.
   - Cai GitHub App Utterances cho repo dtcom-web tai https://github.com/apps/utterances
3) Moi trang dich vu se tu tao mot chu de Issues rieng theo pathname.
4) Khach muon binh luan cong khai can dang nhap GitHub.
5) Neu khach khong dung GitHub, ho van co the dat cau hoi bang nut "Gui cau hoi qua Zalo".
